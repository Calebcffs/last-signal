Required planning artifact per Whitepaper §12. Every plant in the game, in one place, written **before** final transmission prose (per the dependency chain in `04-narrative-beatsheet.md`) so signal text gets written *against* this table, not the other way around.

**Format rule (fixed by `01-canon-and-endings.md` §3):** every row must have an entry in *Innocent reading at first encounter* — a plausible non-sinister explanation available to the player the moment they see it. If a clue can't clear that bar, cut it or weaken it; an unexplainable clue reads as authorial cheating, not foreshadowing (Whitepaper §12).
